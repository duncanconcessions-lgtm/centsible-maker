Hi, I'm Duncan. I print on an **Anycubic Kobra S1** with the **ACE Pro** multicolor unit, running a **0.4 mm hardened steel nozzle** and mostly **0.2 mm layers**. I love silk filament and print-in-place designs like flexi dragons.

CentsibleMaker is where I write down what actually works (and what doesn't) on my own machine. I also sell designs and printables at my [CentsibleSheetz Etsy shop]({{ site.etsy_url }}).
